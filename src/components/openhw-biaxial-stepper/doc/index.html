Biaxial Stepper Motor

Actuator

A dual-axis (biaxial) stepper motor with concentric shafts, allowing independent rotation of an inner and outer hand. Useful for gauges, clocks, or dashboard instruments.

Pinout

A1+, A1-, B1+, B1-Outer Motor Coils
A2+, A2-, B2+, B2-Inner Motor Coils

Usage

Requires two separate stepper motor drivers (e.g., A4988) to control both axes independently.
